# G06-06C2b — Direction accepted; metadata-only first implementation

2026-10-30. Reviewed design/report SHA256 `25136b0c6cc61d3661d0fbef3d437d40fccc4cf840a07f1a47bebc7512f82afc`. C2a accepted separately. Supplier architecture is **DIRECTION_ACCEPTED_WITH_BINDING_CORRECTIONS**, not approval of the proposed evidence DTO/adapter or CFR classification algorithm.

## Binding corrections / answers Q1–Q3

1. **Q1 rejected:** neither equal nor near-equal header rates plus nb_frames proves CFR. No threshold 0.001 or 1e-5 may set is_cfr=True. FFmpeg itself describes r_frame_rate as a guessed base rate, not a per-frame cadence guarantee. Header rate difference alone also cannot claim CONFIRMED_VFR. First parser retains both rates separately and makes no CFR determination. [Official AVStream definitions](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/master/libavformat/avformat.h).
2. **Packet/frames distinction:** reject design rule total_frames=packet_count and variance(packet duration)=0 ⇒ confirmed CFR. ffprobe exposes separate packet/frame records and separate count options; a packet result is not automatically frame presentation evidence. Bounded/partial scans cannot prove the entire clip. A future verifier needs explicit coverage, EOF/completion, stream identity and appropriate presentation timestamp/count policy; its algorithm remains unapproved. [Official ffprobe options](https://www.ffmpeg.org/ffprobe.html#Main-options).
3. **Q2 direction accepted, implementation deferred:** source-bound versioned cache should avoid repeat work, but stat validation is a freshness check, not absolute content/authenticity proof. Bind full digest, selected stream, probe/verification policy/version and completion scope, not only basename/path or ClipEntry partial hash. Do not mandate a new repository-local .cache or implement retry/rescan transparently now. Path/identity and IO supplier belong to a later slice.
4. **Q3 fail-closed for geometry accepted:** unknown/VFR cannot silently become verified CFR or cause omission of primary cuts under D02. Transcoding is future explicit workflow with storage/ownership implications, not an implemented one-click action. Header parser does not block the entire analysis UI or mutate proposals; it only preserves reported metadata.
5. Matrix must separate converter math inputs from future source evidence. Converter needs rational rates, exact frame count and caller CFR flag; codec name, path/hash/provenance, camera TC/time_base are not extra required parameters of its existing API. Proposed SourceFrameEvidence lists 12 fields, not 11; inconsistent naming/counts and mutable provenance must not become an implementation contract by accident.
6. Remove unmeasured claims of '<50ms', '15–45min', universal '40–120GB', inevitable drift after '1–2min', 100% coverage or '<0.5s'. Full digest does not provide absolute proof against every replacement race. av_read_frame is packet reading; do not label it full frame decoding. Keep metadata import bug source-grounded without claiming every manifest everywhere has those fallback values.

## Approved first slice G06-06C2b-I

Two pure new modules: `source_frame_evidence_contracts.py` and `source_frame_evidence_parser.py`, but their actual deliverable is **VideoStreamHeader + parse_ffprobe_video_header** only, six fields of reported metadata. No SourceFrameEvidence/CFREvidenceLevel, no is_cfr flag, confidence or permission status; no quantize adapter, no supplier/subprocess/cache/filesystem/identity inspection. Header data remains unverified even when all fields are populated.

Exact APIs, limits, unknown handling, stream selection and finite tests are in the archived workorder. Implement those corrections and update the design in the same task; do not spend another design-only round restating them. Missing actual producer/evidence/geometry composition remain visible deferred dependencies, not done features.
